# Stone AI -- Competitive Analysis for the Russian/CIS AI Market

**Date:** March 21, 2026
**Subject:** stoneai.ru -- 50+ AI models, Telegram + Web, per-token billing

---

## Executive Summary

The Russian AI aggregator market in 2026 is crowded and growing rapidly, with 20+ active platforms competing for users who cannot directly access Western AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) due to sanctions and payment restrictions. Stone AI's core differentiators -- BYOK, 4 payment methods (Stars/TON/Lava/Heleket), Telegram-native experience, and per-token transparency -- are strong, but the platform needs aggressive SEO and content marketing to break through in a market dominated by established players like BotHub, MashaGPT, ChadAI, and GPTunnel.

## 1. Competitive Landscape -- Detailed Profiles

### 1.1 Russian AI Aggregators (Direct Competitors)

#### BotHub (bothub.chat) -- MAJOR COMPETITOR
- **Models:** GPT-5.2, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini, DeepSeek, Midjourney, video/audio generation. Broadest model selection.
- **Pricing:** Pay-as-you-go ("Caps" internal currency). Packages: 200 rub (1M chars), 600 rub (3M), 1500 rub (7.5M), 5400 rub (30M). Free tier: 10,000 chars.
- **Payment:** Russian cards, crypto, Qiwi, YuMoney -- covers Russian market well.
- **Telegram:** Yes, has a Telegram bot.
- **Web:** Full web app with chat interface.
- **SEO:** Strong. Habr articles, vc.ru presence, active PR. Appears in aggregator rankings consistently in top-3.
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Wider brand recognition, Google Play app, legal entity mentions (news coverage). OpenAI API integration, specialized tools (legal doc search via GARANT).
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** No BYOK. No TON/Stars payment. No Telegram Mini App (just a bot). Internal currency ("caps") is less transparent than USD per-token.

#### MashaGPT (mashagpt.ru) -- MAJOR COMPETITOR
- **Models:** ChatGPT 5.4, Claude Sonnet/Opus, Gemini Pro, Grok, Midjourney, Flux, Kling, Suno. Multi-modal (text/image/video/audio).
- **Pricing:** Subscription model. Free: 7 req/day. Base: 990 rub/mo (20M tokens). Ultra: 1990 rub/mo (50M). Pro: 19990 rub/mo (700M). Annual discounts 16-52%.
- **Payment:** Visa, Mastercard, MIR, SBP. All in rubles.
- **Telegram:** Has a Telegram channel for updates, no full Telegram bot/app.
- **Web:** Full web app + desktop apps (Win/Mac) + mobile (iOS/Android) + Chrome extension. Strong multi-platform presence.
- **SEO:** VERY STRONG. Blog with SEO-optimized articles ("besplatnye-neyroseti-online-2026", "neyroset-dlya-reklamy-yandex-direct-2026"). Ranks for "ChatGPT на русском без VPN". Multiple landing pages.
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Multi-platform (web, desktop, mobile, extension). Strong blog/SEO. Established brand. Business tiers (Ultra/Enterprise).
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** Subscription-only (no per-token). No BYOK. No Telegram bot. No crypto payments. Intrusive Telegram subscription popups (user complaint).

#### ChadAI / ChadGPT (chadgpt.ru) -- MAJOR COMPETITOR
- **Models:** GPT 5.2, Grok 4, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude 4.5, DeepSeek V3.2, Midjourney v7, Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling 2.6 Pro, Suno AI v5.
- **Pricing:** Subscription from 290 rub/mo ("Mini", "Opti", "Plus" tiers). Business: 1.6 rub per 1000 words. Heavily promoted with discount promo codes (up to 40% off).
- **Payment:** Russian cards. In Ministry of Digital Development registry (official legitimacy).
- **Telegram:** Yes, has a Telegram bot.
- **Web:** Full web interface.
- **SEO:** Moderate. Promo code aggregators drive significant traffic. Official registry listing helps trust.
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Government registry listing (trust factor). Aggressive promo code marketing. Video/music generation. Referral system.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** Subscription-only. No BYOK. No crypto/TON payments. Unused balance expires monthly.

#### GPTunnel (gptunnel.ru) -- DIRECT COMPETITOR (Similar Model)
- **Models:** 100+ models including ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Gemini, DeepSeek.
- **Pricing:** Per-token pay-as-you-go. Starting from 2.8 rub per 1K tokens. No subscriptions. Min top-up: $5.
- **Payment:** SBP, bank cards, cryptocurrency.
- **Telegram:** Not prominent.
- **Web:** Full web app.
- **SEO:** Moderate. Habr comparisons, vc.ru mentions. Positioned as "honest pay-as-you-go."
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** MOST SIMILAR PRICING MODEL. Claims 100+ models. API access for developers. Established user base.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** No BYOK. No Telegram Mini App or Stars/TON. Less transparent pricing structure. No free tier mentioned.

#### SyntxAI (syntx.ai) -- STRONG COMPETITOR (Telegram-first)
- **Models:** 90+ neural networks. Veo 3, Sora 2 Pro, Kling, MiniMax, Midjourney, Flux, Suno, ChatGPT, Claude.
- **Pricing:** From 890 rub/mo. Ultra Elite: 10,191 rub/mo (unlimited premium access). Subscription-based.
- **Pricing:** Subscription tiers.
- **Telegram:** PRIMARY CHANNEL. @syntx_bot. 800,000+ monthly users.
- **Web:** syntx.ai web interface available.
- **SEO:** Strong vc.ru presence. Reviewed on multiple tech blogs. Featured in "best Telegram AI bots" lists.
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Telegram-first like Stone AI, but with 800K+ users. Largest Telegram bot in this space. Strong media coverage.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** Subscription-only. No BYOK. No per-token billing. No TON/Stars/crypto payments.

#### EpicAI (epicai.ru) -- COMPETITOR
- **Models:** ChatGPT, Claude 4.6, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Llama, Mistral, Midjourney, DALL-E, Sora, Pika, Suno, Runway, Kling, Flux, etc.
- **Pricing:** Token packages. Starter 498 rub (500K tokens), Basic 946 rub (1M), Pro 1792 rub (2M), Premium 4233 rub (5M), Enterprise 7968 rub (10M). Free: 20K tokens + daily 5K login bonus. Tokens never expire.
- **Payment:** Russian cards.

- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Tokens never expire. Daily login bonuses (engagement loop). Some free unlimited models (GPT-5 nano, Qwen 3.5). Clean pricing page.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** No BYOK. No Telegram presence. No crypto/TON/Stars. Token-based (not transparent USD per-token).

#### GoGPT (gogpt.ru) -- COMPETITOR

- **Pricing:** From 699 rub. Free tier available (GPT-3.5 fallback). Premium needed for GPT-5.

### 1.2 Russian Big Tech AI (Indirect Competitors)

#### GigaChat (Sber) -- giga.chat
- **Models:** GigaChat Lite, Pro, Max (proprietary). Text, image, audio generation.
- **Pricing:** Freemium. 1M free tokens for individuals. Paid packages available. API prices reduced 3x in Feb 2026. Pay-as-you-go available.
- **Payment:** Sber ecosystem (SberPay, Russian cards). Full RU payment support.
- **Telegram:** Kandinsky bot (image generation).
- **Web:** Full web app at giga.chat.
- **SEO:** DOMINANT. Sber's brand authority. First-page for "нейросеть бесплатно", "нейросеть Россия".
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Brand trust (Sber). 1M free tokens. Russian-language optimization. Government/enterprise credibility. 3x price reduction shows aggressive pricing. No VPN needed.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** Only proprietary models (no GPT/Claude/Gemini). Closed ecosystem. No multi-model choice. Not competitive on cutting-edge reasoning. No Telegram Mini App.

#### YandexGPT / Alice AI -- yandex.cloud
- **Models:** Alice AI LLM (renamed Oct 2025), YandexGPT 5 Pro, YandexGPT Lite. Text-only (mostly).
- **Pricing:** Free via Alice/Yandex Browser (including Pro model since Jul 2025). API: Alice AI LLM 0.50 rub/1K input, 1.20 rub/1K output. YandexGPT Pro 5.1: 0.80 rub/1K. Lite: 0.20 rub/1K.
- **Payment:** Yandex Cloud billing. Russian cards.
- **Telegram:** Alice integrated into Yandex services, not Telegram-specific.
- **Web:** Integrated into Yandex Browser, ya.ru, Yandex 360.
- **SEO:** DOMINANT. Yandex's own search engine gives preferential positioning.
- **Strengths vs Stone AI:** Free access (even Pro model). Integrated into Russia's #1 search engine. Best Russian language understanding. Enterprise via Yandex Cloud. Massive existing user base.
- **Weaknesses vs Stone AI:** Only Yandex models. No GPT/Claude/Gemini access. No multi-model aggregation. No per-token billing flexibility. API-only for developers. No image/video generation aggregation.

## 5. SWOT Analysis for Stone AI

### Strengths
1. **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)** -- unique among ALL Russian competitors
2. **4 payment methods** -- Stars, TON, Lava (cards/SBP), Heleket (crypto). No competitor matches this.
3. **Telegram Mini App** -- native haptic feedback, Stars integration. Most competitors only have basic bots.
4. **Per-token billing in USD** -- transparent, no subscription lock-in. Only GPTunnel is similar.
5. **50+ models across 5 categories** -- text, image, video, 3D, audio. Most complete multi-modal offering.
6. **3D generation** -- unique feature (Tripo). No other Russian aggregator offers this.
7. **Video generation** -- 5 models (Kling, Runway, Pika, Stable Video, Luma). Competitive.
8. **Audio TTS/STT** -- 9 voices + Whisper. Differentiated offering.
9. **WebChat (ChatGPT-style)** -- sidebar, markdown, code highlight. Premium UX.
10. **15 free requests/day** -- competitive free tier (more than MashaGPT's 7, similar to others).

### Weaknesses
1. **Brand recognition: ZERO** -- not appearing in ANY aggregator ranking list (DTF, vc.ru, Habr, Sostav).
2. **SEO content: minimal** -- only 3 blog articles vs MashaGPT's content library.
3. **No Google Play / App Store presence** -- BotHub has Android app; MashaGPT has all platforms.
4. **No Ministry of Digital Development listing** -- ChadAI has this trust signal.
5. **No promo code system** -- competitors drive significant traffic through promo aggregators.
6. **Pricing may be higher on some models** -- Claude Opus at $130/1M vs ishushka's $100. Some models 50-100% more expensive.
7. **No desktop/Chrome extension** -- MashaGPT covers all platforms.
8. **Railway deploy URL** -- "website-production-907e.up.railway.app" not optimal for SEO. Need stoneai.ru domain.

### Opportunities
1. **First-mover on BYOK in Russia** -- no competitor offers this. Position as "bring your own key, save money."
2. **Telegram Stars + TON** -- growing crypto/Web3 audience in Russia can be captured.
3. **3D generation niche** -- completely unoccupied in Russia. "3D модель из фото" has search volume.
4. **Per-token transparency** -- market is shifting from subscriptions. GPTunnel validated this model.
5. **vc.ru / DTF / Habr organic content** -- free distribution channels with massive reach.
6. **Video AI content boom** -- "нейросеть для видео" is rapidly growing search term.
7. **B2B/API market** -- GPTunnel/AiTunnel show demand. Stone AI's API docs are a start.
8. **Aggregator ranking articles** -- get listed in the "Top 24 aggregators" articles on DTF/vc.ru.

### Threats
1. **GigaChat 3x price reduction** -- Sber is aggressively pricing down. Free tier of 1M tokens is generous.
2. **YandexGPT free Pro access** -- Yandex made Pro model free in Alice. Hard to compete on price with free.
3. **SyntxAI's 800K users** -- dominant Telegram position already captured.
4. **MashaGPT's SEO dominance** -- massive content library, multi-platform presence.
5. **Regulatory risk** -- Russia may regulate AI aggregators or require data localization.
6. **OpenRouter dependency** -- single point of failure for model access.
7. **ChatGPT Go plan ($5/mo)** -- OpenAI introduced a cheap tier; some users may use VPN + Go instead.
8. **Market saturation** -- 24+ aggregators listed in DTF rankings alone.
